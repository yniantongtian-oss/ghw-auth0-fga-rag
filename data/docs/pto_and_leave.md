# Time Off and Leave

This leave policy covers every Northline employee.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 22 days of paid vacation each calendar year. Up to 5 unused days carry into the next year. Part-time employees receive a prorated balance.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 8 paid sick days a year. Sick days are not paid out when you leave the company.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Leave can start up to two weeks before a due date or placement date.

## Holidays

Northline closes from 24 December through 1 January. Those closure days do not deduct the 22 vacation days.

## How to book

Book vacation in the HR system at least two weeks ahead for a block longer than three days. Your manager approves the request. People Operations can see the calendar; the request does not grant access to compensation files.
