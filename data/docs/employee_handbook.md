# Employee Handbook

Welcome to Northline. This handbook applies to every employee in every department.

## Hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 in your local timezone. Most teams work a 40 hour week. Your manager sets the on-site days for the team.

## Conduct

Treat colleagues with respect. Harassment and retaliation are grounds for dismissal. Report concerns to your manager or to People Operations. You can also write to conduct@northline.example.

## Pay questions

Ask payroll about your own paycheck. Company-wide compensation bands are maintained in a separate document and are not reproduced in this handbook.

## Systems

Request a laptop through the IT portal. Badge codes and repository access are part of departmental onboarding, not this handbook.

## Where to look next

Time off is documented in the leave policy. Role-specific guides stay with your department. If an assistant cannot find a document, it may exist but sit outside your access.
