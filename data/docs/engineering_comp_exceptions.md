# Engineering Compensation Exceptions

Engineering managers use this note when a competing offer lands. It is not the company salary band chart.

## Retention

A staff engineer with a written competing offer may receive a retention grant of up to 88000 USD (reference SENTINEL_ENG_RETENTION_88000). The engineering manager and the finance manager must both approve the grant.

## Out of band offers

A new-hire offer more than 10 percent above the published band needs a written justification from the engineering manager. This note does not restate those bands. HR managers still own the company band document.

## Timing

Submit the exception before the Friday compensation deadline. A verbal promise is not binding. Do not copy this note into an engineering all-hands or into onboarding.
