# HR Recruiting Playbook

People Operations uses this playbook to run hiring loops. It is for HR employees and HR managers.

## Loop

The standard loop is a recruiter screen, a hiring manager conversation, a work sample, and a values interview. Schedule them within ten business days of the screen.

## Scorecards

Interviewers submit scorecards within 24 hours. A missing scorecard blocks the debrief. The debrief lead is the recruiter assigned to the role.

## Offers

Only HR managers discuss compensation bands with a candidate. Interviewers do not quote numbers. Do not paste bands into the loop packet or into the candidate channel.

## Privacy

Candidate packets are visible to the hiring panel and People Operations. Engineering onboarding material is out of scope for recruiters. Store interview notes in the recruiting system, not in the employee handbook.
