# Salary Bands 2026

These compensation bands are effective 1 January 2026. Distribution is limited to people who hold the HR manager role.

## Engineering base salary

The L3 engineer base salary is 118000 USD.
The L4 engineer base salary is 148000 USD.
The L5 engineer base salary is 186000 USD.
The L6 engineering base salary band is 242000 USD (reference SENTINEL_SALARY_L6_242000).
The L7 engineer base salary is 275000 USD.

## Product and design

The L5 product manager base salary is 176000 USD.
The L6 product manager base salary is 228000 USD.
The L5 designer base salary is 162000 USD.

## Equity

Target equity for an L6 engineer is 40 percent of base salary, vesting over four years with a one-year cliff. Refresh grants are decided each March by HR managers.

## Process

HR managers enter the band in the offer letter. Recruiters, engineering managers, finance, and the chief executive do not receive this document through their other roles. A general employee must not see these figures in the HR assistant.
