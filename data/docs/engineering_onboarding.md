# Engineering Onboarding

Read this during your first week on an engineering team.

## First day

Your engineering badge code is ENG-BADGE-4419. Pick up the laptop from IT cage B before standup. The temporary login slip is in the badge envelope.

## Access

Join the GitHub organization northline-eng. Your mentor submits the access request on day one. Production credentials wait until security training sec-101 is complete.

## First week

Complete sec-101 by day 5. Ship a docs-only pull request so the review bot knows your account. Read the on-call shadow notes pinned in the engineering channel.

## Who helps

Your onboarding buddy is listed in the team directory. Engineering managers approve repository exceptions. Compensation questions still go to People Operations; this guide does not list salary bands.
