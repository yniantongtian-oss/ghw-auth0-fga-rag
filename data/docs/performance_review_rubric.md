# Performance Review Rubric

Managers use this rubric during the spring review cycle. Individual contributors do not score themselves with these labels.

## Ratings

1. Exceptional. Repeatedly changes the trajectory of the team.
2. Strong. Exceeds the expectations of the role.
3. Meeting. Solid, reliable delivery against the plan.
4. Developing. Progress is visible but uneven.
5. Off track. Needs a formal plan within 30 days.

## Calendar

Managers submit ratings by 15 March. Calibration meetings are manager-only. Notes from calibration are not copied into the employee handbook.

## Evidence

Quote shipped work or closed roles. Do not include medical information or protected leave in a rating justification. The rating a manager submits is visible to the manager's manager and to HR managers after calibration, through the review tool, not through this rubric.
